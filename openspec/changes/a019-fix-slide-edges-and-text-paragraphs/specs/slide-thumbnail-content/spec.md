## Purpose

Operators need to see every edge of the projected slide while browsing either upper thumbnail list.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Full slide within the thumbnail frame

Both upper PowerPoint lists SHALL preserve the slide aspect ratio and keep the four source edges visible inside the frame, clear of selection, next-slide and number markers.

#### Scenario: Move selection and resize a panel

- **WHEN** the operator browses 4:3 or 16:9 slides and changes the selection or thumbnail width
- **THEN** the whole source remains visible with its slide number and the current marker.
